# Retail Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to understand business performance, sales trends, product profitability, customer behaviour, and store performance.

The project uses Python for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Power BI to create an interactive business dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Power BI

## Dataset Information
- Approximately 12,000 sales transactions
- Data covers 2023–2025
- Includes customer, product, store, sales, payment, and profit information.

## Analysis Performed
### Python Analysis
- Data loading and inspection
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Yearly and monthly sales trends
- Product and category performance
- Regional and store performance
- Customer demographic analysis
- Payment method and sales channel analysis

### Power BI Dashboard
Two interactive dashboard pages were created:

1. **Executive Overview**
   - Total Revenue
   - Total Profit
   - Total Orders
   - Profit Margin
   - Total Quantity
   - Monthly Revenue Trend
   - Region and Category Performance
   - Payment Method and Sales Channel Analysis
   - Top 10 Products by Revenue

2. **Product & Customer Insights**
   - Top 10 Products by Profit
   - Category-wise Profit
   - Sub-Category Performance
   - Top 10 Customers by Revenue
   - Gender-wise Revenue
   - Revenue by Age Group

## Key Business Insights
- Sales increased by 6.75% in 2024.
- Sales declined by 7.19% in 2025.
- North region generated the highest revenue and profit.
- S10 Chennai recorded the highest store revenue.
- S08 Ahmedabad recorded the lowest store revenue.
- Standard stores contributed 74.04% of total revenue.
- Flagship stores contributed 25.96% of total revenue.
- Middle Age customers generated the highest revenue among age groups.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate the sales decline in 2025.
- Study successful strategies in the North region.
- Improve the performance of low-performing stores.
- Focus on profitable products and customer segments.
- Monitor monthly sales and profit trends.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Python and Power BI can be used together to analyze retail business data and generate meaningful insights to support business decision-making.
